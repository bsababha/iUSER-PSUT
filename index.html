Our approach

Build boldly.
Validate honestly.

The lab brings together students, researchers, and partners to move ideas beyond simulation. We value rigorous engineering, open collaboration, and systems that are useful outside the lab.

Evidence over hype

Measure performance, document limitations, and let results lead.

Human-centered autonomy

Design systems that improve safety, access, and capability.

Prototype to learn

Share the signal

Lab Director

Where engineering meets autonomy

Belal H. Sababha, Ph.D. · Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering

Dr. Sababha leads iUSER Lab — Intelligent Unmanned Systems Engineering Research Lab — at Princess Sumaya University for Technology. He is an academic and drones and embedded-systems consultant whose work connects autonomous systems, embedded control, machine vision, and dependable engineering with real-world applications.

He joined PSUT in 2012 and previously worked as a Senior Controls Engineer in Chrysler Group's Powertrain Controls department in Michigan. He earned his Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering — Embedded Systems from Oakland University in 2011.
